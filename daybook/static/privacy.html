Daybook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Daybook is a private daily diary. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, username and email address.
  • Your password, stored only as a one-way hash (PBKDF2-SHA256 with a unique salt). Nobody, including us, can read your actual password.
  • Your diary entries: the date, title, text and mood you write.
  • Sign-in records: which devices are signed in (as hashed tokens that expire after 30 days) and short-lived 6-digit verification codes (hashed, deleted after use or 5 minutes).

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or analytics, and the app contains no ads or third-party trackers.

How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in securely.
  • To email you verification codes when you sign up, sign in or reset your password.
  • To store your diary entries and show them back to you.

We never sell your information, share it with advertisers, or read your diary entries.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in Daybook's database on a cloud server and is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Verification emails are delivered through Google's Gmail service.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and entries until you delete them. Deleted entries and deleted accounts are removed from the database immediately.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all your diary entries at any time: open Daybook, tap your profile circle, choose Delete account and confirm with your password. You can do this in the Android app or on the Daybook website. Consider using Export backup first if you want to keep a copy of your writing.

Children

Daybook is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
